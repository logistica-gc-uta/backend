## Accepted contract
- Order fields: `deliveryLat`, `deliveryLng`; retain `deliveryAddress`, zone, schedule and relations.
- Zone fields: `depotLat`, `depotLng`; use existing zone entity.
- Persist nullable numeric pairs, no defaults or fabricated backfill; historical rows remain identifiable by both NULL values.
- Human selected optional coordinates in new creations for backward compatibility. When supplied, both values must be valid finite numbers, latitude [-90,90], longitude [-180,180]. Explicit malformed/incomplete pairs must be rejected, not silently discarded.
- Caller must select and confirm the map marker; backend neither geocodes nor proves user map interaction. Seed/fixture coordinates must be clearly labelled test data.
- Pair/range integrity is enforced in DTOs, services and PostgreSQL where supported. Never claim to detect a swap whose numbers remain valid in both ranges.
- Planning requires valid destination AND corresponding zone depot; expose an explicit read-model/eligibility contract mapped to `GeoPoint { lat, lng }`, without invoking or changing Clarke & Wright. No silent omission of historical records.
- Retain all PR #19 security policies and checks, especially DRIVER status writes returning 403 until #10. No role changes, optimization endpoints, assignments, route states or geocoding.
